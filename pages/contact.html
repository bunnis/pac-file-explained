How to reach me

PAC Explained is maintained by abskulaity. The best way to reach me depends on what you need:

  • Email: bunnis@users.noreply.github.com — for general questions, feedback, or anything that isn't suited to a public issue.
  • Website: abskulaity.net — more about me and other projects.
  • GitHub: open an issue or a pull request — the fastest path for reporting an error in the reference or suggesting new content.

Reporting a correction

Accuracy matters more than anything else on this site. If something is wrong, outdated, or unclear, please tell me — and if you can, include the correct information and a source (a spec, the MDN page, or a reproducible test with pacparser). That makes it much faster to verify and publish the fix.

I read every message, but this is a side project maintained in spare time, so replies may take a few days.
